# 👥 SangSmart — User Persona

## About User Persona

User Persona is a representation of the target users of SangSmart, created to understand their characteristics, backgrounds, needs, frustrations, and daily activities.

SangSmart is designed to support banana farmers and other users involved in horticulture by providing agricultural education, plant monitoring, weather information, pest and disease detection, AI chatbot assistance, and expert consultation.

This user persona documentation helps identify user needs and serves as a foundation for designing an effective, accessible, and user-centered application.

---

## 🎯 User Persona Overview

SangSmart identifies four main user personas:

1. **Desi Maulida, S.P., M.Si.** — Horticulture Lecturer
2. **Bu Fitri** — Banana Farmer and Entrepreneur
3. **Tri Sanjayani** — Horticulture Student
4. **Pak Somad** — Agricultural Entrepreneur and Farmer

---

## 1. Desi Maulida, S.P., M.Si.

**Role:** Lecturer

**Location:** Lampung, Indonesia

**Education:** Politeknik Negeri Lampung

**Background:**

A lecturer in the Horticultural Crop Production Technology study program who is also involved in academic activities and horticultural education.

**Needs:**
- An effective application that can help reduce farmers' workload.
- Easy-to-use plant monitoring features.
- Accessible agricultural information.

**Frustrations:**
- Difficulties in handling pests and diseases affecting banana plants.
- Limited agricultural knowledge among banana farmers.

**Everyday Activities:**

Teaching students in the Horticulture Study Program at Politeknik Negeri Lampung.

---

## 2. Bu Fitri

**Age:** 55

**Role:** Banana Farmer and Entrepreneur

**Location:** Lampung, Indonesia

**Marital Status:** Married

**Background:**

A banana farmer who manages approximately three hectares of land around her home with her husband. She cultivates bananas using organic materials without chemical fertilizers and sells her agricultural products locally.

**Needs:**
- An easy-to-use agricultural application.
- Automated watering and plant monitoring features.
- Clear information about pests, diseases, and weather conditions.

**Frustrations:**
- Difficulties adapting to modern agricultural technology.
- Manual watering requires considerable effort.
- Difficulty detecting pests and unpredictable weather conditions.
- Limited time and labor for managing the plantation.

**Everyday Activities:**

Taking care of banana plants and selling agricultural products, including bananas and banana leaves.

---

## 3. Tri Sanjayani

**Age:** 21

**Role:** Student

**Education:** Politeknik Negeri Lampung — Horticultural Crop Production Technology

**Location:** Metro, Lampung, Indonesia

**Marital Status:** Single

**Background:**

A student in the Horticultural Crop Production Technology program with experience contributing to banana cultivation projects, including developing quality banana seedlings using smart farming, IoT technology, and tissue culture.

**Needs:**
- Practical agricultural guidance and proven solutions.
- Information to optimize harvest yields.
- Knowledge to maintain banana plant health.

**Frustrations:**
- Difficulty finding integrated agricultural information.
- Lack of accessible guidance for solving field problems, such as pests, diseases, and fertilizer selection.

**Everyday Activities:**

Learning and practicing horticultural cultivation techniques, particularly banana farming.

---

## 4. Pak Somad

**Age:** 75

**Role:** Farmer and Agricultural Entrepreneur

**Location:** Lampung, Indonesia

**Marital Status:** Married

**Background:**

An experienced farmer who manages agricultural land with various crops, including bananas. His daily activities involve planting, watering, maintaining, and harvesting crops. He has years of experience selecting quality seedlings but still faces agricultural challenges.

**Needs:**
- An application with clear and easy-to-understand information.
- Practical agricultural guidance that can be applied directly.
- Agricultural knowledge that can be easily shared with the farming community.

**Frustrations:**
- Difficulties managing unpredictable weather conditions.
- Challenges in handling diseases affecting banana plants.

**Everyday Activities:**

Managing agricultural land and maintaining crops, from planting to harvesting.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the four user personas, several important insights were identified:

| User Needs | SangSmart Solutions |
|---|---|
| Agricultural knowledge | Agricultural Education (Articles & Videos) |
| Easy plant monitoring | Plant Monitoring Dashboard |
| Weather information | Weather Information |
| Pest and disease identification | Pest and Disease Detection |
| Instant agricultural assistance | AI Chatbot |
| Professional agricultural guidance | Expert Consultation |

## 🚀 Design Implications

These personas serve as the foundation for SangSmart's UI/UX design decisions.

The application focuses on:

- **Accessibility:** Providing a simple and user-friendly interface for users with different levels of technological experience.
- **Education:** Delivering relevant banana farming knowledge through articles and educational videos.
- **Monitoring:** Helping farmers monitor plant conditions and agricultural activities.
- **Assistance:** Providing AI chatbot support and direct consultation with agricultural experts.
- **Efficiency:** Helping farmers access useful agricultural information and manage their farming activities more effectively.

---

## 📌 Conclusion

The SangSmart user personas represent different user groups, including farmers, agricultural entrepreneurs, lecturers, and students.

By understanding their needs and frustrations, SangSmart aims to create a user-centered agricultural application that makes banana farming smarter, more accessible, and more efficient.
